# Product: ProFix 360 Solutions LLC website

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages. Only external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts. No frameworks, no tracking.

## Users
- **Homeowners in New Jersey** who need a camera system, network cable, painting, lighting, small repairs or Christmas lights put up.
- **Restaurant and office owners/managers in New Jersey** who need the same trades done in a business space (cameras and CAT6 especially).
- Both mostly arrive from a Facebook post or group, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- Secondary user: the business owner, who receives the site free and should recognise their own business, done better than they imagined.

## Purpose
Turn a curious visitor into a free-estimate request through the owner's real channel, Facebook Messenger, following the owner's own process: "Send a few pictures and your town so we can discuss your project."

## Positioning
One local outfit for six trades that usually need six contractors: security cameras, CAT6 network cabling, interior & exterior painting, LED & low-voltage lighting, handyman repairs, Christmas lights. Serves homes and businesses. (Source: owner's 2026-10-20 Facebook group ad.)

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact is Facebook Messenger only. No phone, no email published (brief, 2026-10-20).
- The page cannot receive photos itself; the estimate form writes the message and opens Messenger, where the visitor attaches photos.
- No work photos, reviews, prices, owner name, hours, years in business or towns are published. None appear on the page.
- Logo: gold lion-in-shield with "PROFIX 360 SOLUTIONS" wordmark on black (Facebook profile image).

## Brand commitments
- Name: "ProFix 360 Solutions LLC" as primary (brief instruction); "ProFix 360 Solutions" acceptable short form.
- Owner's words, verbatim: "From restaurants and offices to homes—clean work, fair pricing, and attention to detail." and "Message us for a FREE estimate!"
- Voice: "we / our" (LLC, page speaks as "us"). Casual, local, plain.
- Colours from the logo: gold accent; ground is navy, never black.

## Evidence on hand
- Six verbatim services and the promise line (group ad, 2026-10-20).
- Facebook profile image (logo), page tagline "Security Cameras • Low Voltage • LED lighting".
- Nothing else: no photos of work, no reviews.

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first phone screen says who, what, where and how to get a free estimate.
2. Only real facts; gaps are left out, not invented.
3. Match the owner's process exactly: pictures + town, by Messenger.
4. Speak to both homes and businesses.
